#4471 · two date failures

owner hjmro documented reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

Ticket #4471 merged 412 unique reports, but its reports describe two independent date failures. Grouping uses the tenant's locale and booking form rather than the intake tag.

These are two distinct failures, recorded separately in parser issue #1 and hint issue #2.

Problems found

Parser reads day-first dates as month-first

The shared parser read every date month first. In a day-first tenant, a date such as 4/7 was interpreted as 7 April instead of 4 July. This group contains 379 reports.

booking-v3 gives day-first tenants a month-first hint

booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY. Its users typed dates in that order, while the tenant expected day-first input. This was a separate form-hint failure affecting 33 reports.
