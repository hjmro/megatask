Ticket #4471 merged 412 unique reports, but its reports describe two independent date failures. Grouping uses the tenant's locale and booking form rather than the intake tag.

The existing parser ticket covers the larger group. The booking-v3 hint needs its own ticket.

Problems found

Parser reads day-first dates as month-first

The shared parser reads every date month first. In a day-first tenant, a date such as 4/7 is interpreted as 7 April instead of 4 July. This group contains 379 reports.

booking-v3 gives day-first tenants a month-first hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY. Its users type dates in that order, while the tenant expects day-first input. This is a separate form-hint failure affecting 33 reports.
